Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Idea Creator ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes mobile applications and games (collectively, the "Apps"), including but not limited to Can't Catch, Two or More!, and Emoji Formula. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user privacy across our Apps.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy and design our Apps so that they can be used without providing any personal information:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical location, photos, contacts, or device identifiers).
  • No Analytics or Behavioral Tracking: Our Apps do not integrate third-party analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs to monitor or profile your activity.
  • Local Storage Only: Any app preferences, settings, or game progress generated while using our Apps are stored strictly locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external server.

2. Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not share user data with third parties. Please note that the app distribution platforms through which you download our Apps (such as Google Play or the Apple App Store) may independently collect basic diagnostic, crash, or transaction data in accordance with their own Privacy Policies.

3. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from any users, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

4. Data Security and Retention

Since no user data is collected or transmitted to our servers, there is no risk of your personal information being exposed from our systems. You can remove all locally stored app data at any time by clearing the App's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or our Apps. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or any of our Apps, please contact us:

隐私政策

最近更新日期： 2026年11月24日

Idea Creator（以下简称“我们”）开发并发布移动应用程序与游戏（统称“本系列应用”），包括但不限于 Can't Catch、Two or More! 及 Emoji Formula。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们在您使用本系列应用时如何保护您的隐私。

1. 我们不收集任何信息

我们高度重视您的隐私，所有应用在设计上均无需您提供任何个人数据即可正常使用：

  • 不收集个人数据： 我们不会收集、存储、传输或出售任何可识别个人身份的信息（例如您的姓名、电子邮箱、电话号码、地理位置、照片、通讯录或设备标识符）。
  • 无分析与行为追踪： 本系列应用未集成任何第三方统计分析工具、广告网络或追踪类 SDK 来监控或分析您的使用行为。
  • 仅限本地存储： 您在使用应用过程中产生的偏好设置、游戏进度等数据仅保存在您的设备本地，绝不会上传至任何外部服务器。

2. 第三方服务

本系列应用不会向任何第三方共享用户数据。请注意，您下载应用所使用的分发平台（如 Google Play 或 Apple App Store）可能会依据其自身的隐私政策独立收集基础的崩溃诊断或交易信息。

3. 儿童隐私保护

由于我们不收集任何用户的个人信息，因此我们也不会在知情的情况下收集 13 岁以下（或您所在司法管辖区规定的适用年龄以下）儿童的个人数据。

4. 数据安全与保留

由于我们不收集也不向服务器传输任何用户数据，因此不存在您的个人信息从我们服务器泄露的风险。您可以随时通过设备设置清除应用缓存/存储数据，或直接卸载应用来删除所有本地数据。

5. 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会根据法律法规或应用功能的变化不时更新本隐私政策。任何更新均会发布在本页面上，并同步修改顶部的“最近更新日期”。
