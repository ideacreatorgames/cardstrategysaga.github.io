PhotoVaultX - Private Vault

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to PhotoVaultX - Private Vault. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the app on iPhone and iPad devices.

1. Information Collection

The app is designed to protect your personal photos and videos. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal information. The app does not collect: Names, Email addresses, Phone numbers, Photos or videos, Passwords, Device contacts, Location data, Usage analytics linked to your identity, Any other personal information. All of your files remain stored locally on your device.

2. Local Storage and Encryption

Photos and videos imported into the app are stored locally on your device and protected using iOS security mechanisms and app-level password protection. Your private files are never uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party databases by the app.

3. Local Video and Image Compression

Video and image compression is performed entirely on your device. No media files are transmitted to our servers or to third-party services during compression or processing.

4. Password Protection

The app allows users to create a password to protect access to private content stored within the app. Passwords are stored securely on the device and are not transmitted to us. Users are responsible for remembering their passwords. We cannot recover forgotten passwords.

5. Permissions

The app may request access to the following device features only when necessary: Photos Library Access Used for: Importing photos and videos into the app Saving exported or compressed files back to the Photos library File Access Used for: Importing files from the Files app Exporting files to device storage You may manage or revoke permissions at any time in: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security

6. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain non-personal data, including: Device identifiers (such as IDFA) Advertising data App interaction information This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can manage ad tracking permissions in: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?utm_source=chatgpt.com

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect your data stored within the app. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Users are encouraged to use strong passwords and keep their devices protected.

8. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app or on the related policy page.
