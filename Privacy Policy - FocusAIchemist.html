Focus Alchemist: Pomodoro

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Welcome to Focus Alchemist: Pomodoro.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Alchemist: Pomodoro ("the App") handles information when you use the App on supported Apple devices.

Focus Alchemist is designed to help users stay focused and reduce distractions from other apps, games, and websites. Users can create focus sessions, configure allowed apps and websites, complete focus goals, earn virtual potions, cultivate virtual plants, and collect discoveries.

The App is designed so that focus-related data and game progress are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your focus history, whitelist selections, or game progress.

We do not collect, process, or transmit any personal information or user data to our servers or any third parties through the core functionality of the App. All user data remains exclusively on your device.

1. Information Processed by Focus Alchemist

Focus Alchemist may process and store information locally on your device that is necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Focus session duration
  • Focus session status
  • Completed or failed focus sessions
  • Focus statistics and progress
  • User-selected focus settings
  • Whitelist configurations
  • Virtual potions and other in-app rewards
  • Virtual seeds, plants, flowers, and discoveries
  • Inventory and collection progress
  • Game progression and unlocks
  • App preferences and settings

This information is used to operate the App, maintain your progress, calculate rewards, display statistics, and provide focus-related functionality.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

We do not have access to your locally stored focus history, whitelist configuration, virtual inventory, plant collection, or similar local App data.

2. Personal Information We Do Not Collect

Through the core focus and game functionality of Focus Alchemist, we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not collect or store on our servers information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Personal files
  • Messages
  • Precise location
  • Focus history
  • Whitelist selections
  • Plant and reward collection history
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Any other personally identifiable information

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use its core functionality.

3. Screen Time and App Restriction Features

Focus Alchemist may use Apple-provided technologies such as Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity, and related Screen Time APIs to provide focus and distraction-reduction functionality.

With your authorization, these technologies may allow the App to apply restrictions to apps, app categories, or websites during an active focus session.

These capabilities may be used for purposes such as:

  • Restricting selected apps, app categories, or websites during focus sessions
  • Allowing selected apps or websites to remain available through a whitelist
  • Applying the focus rules selected by you
  • Determining whether conditions associated with a focus session have been satisfied
  • Managing restrictions while a focus session is active

Focus Alchemist does not upload your Screen Time selections or whitelist configurations to our servers.

We do not sell this information or use your Screen Time information to create advertising or marketing profiles.

Information obtained through Apple's Screen Time-related APIs is used only as necessary to provide the focus functionality requested by you.

4. Whitelist

Focus Alchemist allows you to select apps, app categories, or websites that may remain available during supported focus modes.

Your whitelist configuration is processed and stored locally on your device and is used only to provide the focus functionality you configure.

We do not upload your whitelist configuration to our servers, sell it, or use it for any purpose other than providing the requested focus functionality.

5. Focus Sessions and Strict Mode

Depending on the focus mode you select, Focus Alchemist may determine whether the conditions required for an active focus session have been maintained.

For example, certain modes may cause a focus session to fail if the App remains away from the foreground for longer than the permitted period.

Information about whether a focus session succeeds or fails is processed locally and is used to calculate your focus progress and in-app rewards.

We do not receive or store this focus-session information on our servers.

6. Device Orientation and Sensor Information

Certain features may use device sensor information to determine whether the device has been placed face down or is in another supported orientation.

This information is processed locally on your device only for the purpose of providing the relevant focus feature.

We do not upload device orientation or motion information to our servers or use it for any purpose other than providing the requested feature.

7. Virtual Rewards, Plants, and Game Progress

Focus Alchemist uses game-like features to encourage users to complete focus sessions.

Successful focus sessions may provide virtual rewards such as potions. These virtual items may be used to cultivate seeds, grow virtual plants and flowers, unlock discoveries, and complete collections within the App.

Information such as:

  • Potion inventory
  • Plant and flower collection
  • Seeds
  • Discoveries
  • Unlocks
  • Game progress

is stored locally on your device.

We do not upload this information to our servers.

Virtual items in Focus Alchemist are part of the App experience and do not represent real-world money, securities, cryptocurrency, or other financial assets.

8. Permissions

Focus Alchemist may request access to certain Apple system features when necessary to provide its functionality.

Screen Time / Family Controls Authorization

The App may request authorization to use Apple's Screen Time-related technologies in order to provide app and website restrictions, whitelist functionality, and focus modes.

You may decline or revoke applicable authorization through controls provided by iOS.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Focus Alchemist may send notifications related to focus sessions, reminders, completed activities, or other relevant App events.

You can manage notification permissions through iOS Settings.

Motion and Device Sensors

When required by features such as face-down detection, Focus Alchemist may use device motion or orientation information.

This information is processed locally for the requested functionality and is not uploaded to our servers.

9. Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit any locally stored Focus Alchemist data.

Core focus and game data described in this Privacy Policy remain exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or shared with any third parties.

10. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Focus Alchemist's focus history, settings, whitelist configuration, virtual rewards, plant collection, and game progress are stored locally on your device.

This information may remain on your device until it is removed through App functionality, reset where such functionality is available, or the App is deleted, subject to Apple's operating system behavior and device backup mechanisms.

Because we do not store this information on our servers, we cannot remotely access, retrieve, restore, or delete it.

11. Data Security

Focus Alchemist is designed to minimize data transmission and keep its core focus and game data exclusively on your device.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic device, storage system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Focus Alchemist does not knowingly collect any personal information from children through its core functionality.

The App does not require users to provide any personal information in order to use its core focus and game features.

13. Your Privacy Choices

You can control certain privacy-related permissions through iOS Settings, including, where applicable:

  • Screen Time-related authorization
  • Notifications
  • Other device permissions requested by the App

You may revoke applicable permissions at any time. Some Focus Alchemist features may no longer function correctly if permissions necessary for those features are revoked.

14. Third-Party Services and Apple Services

Focus Alchemist relies on Apple system frameworks and services to provide certain functionality.

Apple's processing of information through iOS, the App Store, Screen Time technologies, device backups, iCloud, or other Apple services is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Focus Alchemist, legal requirements, Apple platform requirements, or our privacy practices.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on the applicable Privacy Policy page and the "Last Updated" date will be updated.
